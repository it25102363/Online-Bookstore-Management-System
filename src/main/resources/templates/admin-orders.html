Order Management Rules

Orders follow a controlled status management process.

PENDING orders can be CONFIRMED or CANCELLED.

CONFIRMED orders can be SHIPPED or CANCELLED.

SHIPPED orders can be marked as DELIVERED.

DELIVERED orders cannot be modified or deleted.

Only CANCELLED orders can be permanently deleted.
